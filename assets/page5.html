Maybe this is ours.

Maybe string theory isn’t just about the universe being made of invisible strings connecting everything—it’s about how things that seem completely unrelated can still be connected in ways we don’t understand. And sometimes, when I think about us, I feel like that’s exactly what happened. Three, maybe three and a half years ago, we were already part of the same little universe. We walked through the same corridors, sat in the same classrooms, passed each other countless times, and yet somehow, we never spoke. You were just a girl a few benches ahead of me during an exam, and I remember seeing you and thinking you were really pretty. I had no idea that the person I was looking at for just a moment would eventually become someone so important to me. Looking back now, it almost feels like everything had been quietly falling into place long before we even knew it. Every corridor we walked through, every time we passed each other without saying a word, every moment that brought us closer without us realizing it—it all feels like it had a purpose. And then, when the timing was finally right, we started talking. Not too early, not too late. Just at the exact moment we were meant to find each other. And after that, everything seemed to happen so naturally, so perfectly, as if the universe had been patiently arranging the pieces all along and, finally, everything clicked into place. Maybe that's the most beautiful part of our story: we spent so much time unknowingly moving toward each other, and when we finally met, it felt less like the beginning of something and more like everything had finally fallen exactly where it was supposed to.
